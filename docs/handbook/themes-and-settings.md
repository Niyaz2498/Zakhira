# Themes and settings

Settings is short on purpose. Zakhira has few preferences, and the ones it
has are about the app rather than about your work.

## Dark and light

**Settings → Appearance → Theme** switches between Dark and Light. The
change is immediate and is remembered on that device. On the desktop the same
switch also sits at the bottom of the sidebar, so you need not open Settings
for it.

Every screen in both apps follows the theme — the two are a complete palette,
not a dark mode with a few patches.

Two things worth knowing:

- **The theme is per device.** Setting the phone to light does not change the
  desktop. It is a display preference, not part of your account.
- **They start differently.** The phone follows your system appearance the
  first time you open it, then keeps whatever you choose. The desktop always
  starts dark, and likewise keeps your choice after that.

Neither app follows the system automatically once you have picked a theme,
and there is no "match system" option to return to.

## Sync

**Settings → Sync** reports two facts:

- **Last synced** — the time of the most recent successful sync. "Never"
  means this device has not managed one yet.
- **Server** — the backend this app is talking to.

Neither is editable here. The server address is fixed when you sign in.

To sync immediately, use the Dashboard rather than Settings — **Refresh** in
its header on the desktop, and on the phone either the refresh control or a
pull down on the list. Otherwise both apps sync on their own; see
[Overview](overview.md).

If Last synced is stuck in the past, that is the thing to check when a change
made on one device has not appeared on the other.

## Display name (desktop)

**Settings → Profile → Display Name** sets the name the desktop Dashboard
greets you by. Type it and press Enter or **Save**.

It is cosmetic and local to that computer — it is not part of your account,
does not reach the phone, and is cleared when you sign out.

## Updates (desktop)

**Settings → Updates** shows the version you are running and a **Check now**
button.

The desktop app also checks on its own at launch. When an update is waiting,
a banner appears and the button becomes **Restart to update** — the app
downloads it, shows the progress, and relaunches into the new version.

The phone app has no updater in Settings; it is updated through however you
installed it.

## Signing out

**Sign out** is at the bottom of Settings on the phone, and at the bottom of
the sidebar on the desktop. The phone asks you to confirm; the desktop signs
out on the first click, so take care with it.

Signing out clears that device: your saved sign-in, its cached copy of your
groups and tasks, the desktop's display name, and any pending timer alerts
the phone had scheduled.

Nothing in your account is affected. Sign back in and everything returns.

## What is not configurable

There are no settings for notifications, sync frequency, date format, week
start, default task type, or accent colour. The behaviour each of those
would control is described in the chapter it belongs to — the week starting
on Monday in [Dates and the calendar](dates-and-calendar.md), the hourly
timer nudges in [Tracking time](tracking-time.md).
