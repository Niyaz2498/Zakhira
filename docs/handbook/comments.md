# Comments

A task's **comments** are its running log: what happened, when, and why. Open
any task and the Comments section sits below its details.

## Comments versus Notes

Both are free text on a task, and they are for opposite jobs.

**Notes** is the brief — what the task is and what finishing looks like.
There is one of them, you overwrite it when it changes, and it should read as
current. (Notes can only be edited on the desktop.)

**Comments** are the history — ordered entries you add as work happens. You
add to them rather than replacing them, and old entries stay as written.

If you find yourself rewriting Notes to record that something changed, that
belongs in a comment.

## Adding and reading

The composer sits **above** the log, so posting an entry never means scrolling
past the whole history to reach the box. Type, then **Add comment**.

The log reads **newest first**, directly under the box you typed in. Each
entry is headed by its age — *just now*, *12m ago*, *3h ago*, *2d ago* — and
past a week it switches to the actual date, since "23d ago" tells you less
than the day it happened.

An entry that has been changed since it was posted is marked **· edited**.

A comment can be up to 10,000 characters.

## Editing and deleting

Each entry has **Edit** and **Delete**.

Editing replaces the text and marks the entry as edited; the original wording
is not kept.

**Deleting is permanent.** You are asked to confirm, and after that the entry
is gone — it is not archived, hidden or recoverable on another device. This
is the one genuinely destructive action in the comment log, which is why it
asks.

## Closed tasks have locked logs

Once a task is Completed or Scrapped, its comment log is **read-only**. You
cannot add, edit or delete entries, and the composer is replaced by a line
saying the log is locked. Everything already written stays readable.

A closed task is a record of what happened, and a record that can still be
rewritten afterwards is worth less. If you genuinely need to add something,
recover the task (see [Task states and types](task-states-and-types.md)),
comment, and close it again.

## Comments need a connection

Unlike the rest of a task, comments are not held on your device between
visits — they are fetched from your account each time you open the task. With
no connection you will see an error in place of the log, with a **Tap to
retry**.

This is also why a comment you delete is really gone everywhere, rather than
lingering on your other device until it next syncs.
