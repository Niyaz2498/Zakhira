# Ordering your groups

Your groups are listed alphabetically until you say otherwise. You can set the
order yourself, so the work you care about most sits at the top of the Groups
page instead of whichever group happens to start with an early letter.

## Setting the order

**Reorder** sits in the Groups header, beside the **+**. It appears once you
have two top-level groups — with one there is no order to set.

![The reorder screen. General Tasks is pinned above the groups you can
move.](images/reorder-screen-dark.png)

Drag a group by the **grip handle** on its right. The other rows step aside to
show where it will land, and release drops it there.

![Dragging a group upward. The lifted row is outlined, and the group it is
passing has already moved down into the space it
left.](images/reorder-dragging-dark.png)

Only the handle drags. The rest of the row is deliberately inert, so you can
still scroll a long list with your thumb anywhere on it without picking
anything up by accident.

**Done** saves the arrangement. **Cancel** leaves it exactly as it was — until
you press Done, nothing you drag is decided, so you can shuffle the list freely
and back out of it.

Your Groups page then reads in that order:

![The Groups page in the chosen order, with each group's sub-groups beneath
it.](images/groups-ordered-dark.png)

The order belongs to your account rather than to one device, so it is the same
everywhere you are signed in.

## General Tasks is always first

General Tasks is shown in its own **Pinned** section and cannot be moved. It is
the inbox for anything uncategorised, which is only useful if it is where you
expect it to be, so it stays at the top of the list.

## What the order does not cover

**Sub-groups stay alphabetical inside their parent.** The order you set applies
to top-level groups, and each group's sub-groups are listed beneath it by name.

**A new group goes to the bottom**, wherever its name falls in the alphabet.
This is the deliberate choice: once you have arranged a list, a new group
appearing in the middle of it would quietly undo part of that arrangement.
Drag it where you want it.

**A group you have never reordered keeps its alphabetical place.** Arranging
some of your groups does not scramble the rest — the ones you have positioned
come first, in your order, and everything else follows alphabetically below
them. If you have never used Reorder at all, the list is exactly as it always
was.

## Changing your mind

Reorder as often as you like; there is nothing to undo and no history to lose.
Dragging a group is only about where it appears — it does not move the group
into another group, change its tasks, or touch its star rating. To put a group
*inside* another one, use **Move** on the group itself.
